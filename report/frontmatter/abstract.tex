\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

Choosing when to buy and sell stocks is hard, and harder on the Ho Chi Minh City Stock Exchange
(HOSE), where a $\pm7\%$ daily price band, delayed settlement, trading costs and speculative
operators make many strategies that look profitable on paper impossible to trade. This thesis
develops an AI-driven decision support system that turns machine-learning signals into explained
recommendations with position sizes and risk warnings, and evaluates it under the exchange's own
rules.

This pre-thesis report presents the problem, the proposed system and its data foundation. A
survivorship-free daily dataset of 448 HOSE stocks from February 2020 to October 2026 was
collected from the SSI FastConnect API and cleaned. The work exposed defects that a vendor's
success code hides: overlapping result pages, placeholder prices on 7.9\% of stock-sessions and a
corporate-action adjustment that covers only part of the events. Rebuilding the adjustment from
the exchange's reference prices left a single impossible price gap among liquid stocks, and was
confirmed by an independent source in 97--99\% of events. Exploratory analysis shows that the
150 most traded stocks carry 98.5\% of traded value and nearly all reliable data, that the daily
price band truncates the tails of the return distribution, and that the most traded stocks are
strongly correlated. The models will therefore be trained and evaluated on a liquid universe
re-ranked every session.

The next stages build the signal and decision layers, a backtest that enforces HOSE rules and
corrects for multiple testing, an interactive application that replays historical market
scenarios, and a MetaTrader~5 implementation as an independent check.

\bigskip
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} decision support system, machine learning, stock trading,
Vietnamese stock market, data quality, backtesting.
