- Zero-volume rows are 7.9% of stock rows. SSI fills their OHLC with placeholder values (often open near the floor, close at a stale level), so they must be flagged as non-trading sessions and their OHLC discarded — otherwise each one prints a fake band-wide bar that a detector reads as a climax.

## 3. Price steps beyond the -7% band (gap < -11%)

| series | steps |
|---|---|
| A raw | 502 |
| A adjusted (SSI) | 231 |
| B adjusted, all years | 772 |
| B adjusted, 2020+ | 307 |

- Corporate-action days detected in A from a change in `adj_factor`: **1409**.
- Sessions where the exchange set the reference price >11% below the previous close (a large ex-right): **350**, of which SSI's adjusted close absorbs **195**. Where SSI does adjust, the factor step falls on the ex-date itself; where it does not, the drop survives in the adjusted series.
- Raw-series steps are real ex-right drops; on a correct adjusted series they should vanish. Steps left on an adjusted series are basis seams that a Wyckoff detector would misread as climaxes.
